# Evidence hierarchy

## Level 1 — official and regulatory
Examples: HESA, Office for Students, GOV.UK/MHCLG, ICO, Coventry City Council.

Used for population, regulation, affordability context and local market structure.

## Level 2 — peer-reviewed scholarship
Used to explain mechanisms and debates around:
- information asymmetry and trust;
- online accommodation search;
- PBSA supply and affordability;
- planning/regulation;
- student housing as home and wellbeing;
- two-sided platform economics.

Each study's context and transfer limitation is recorded in `literature_matrix.csv`.

## Level 3 — first-party accommodation evidence
Examples: Coventry University and iQ Student Accommodation.

Used for current room prices, contract length, bills and payment/booking terms.

## Level 4 — live competitor/platform evidence
Examples: Student.com, University Living, Rightmove, SpareRoom and UCAS.

Used to identify existing capabilities and residual gaps. These sources are dynamic and are treated as feature observations rather than stable market facts.

## Level 5 — specialist market intelligence
Examples: Knight Frank and Cushman & Wakefield.

Used to triangulate supply/investment conditions. Commercial reports are not treated as equivalent to official statistics.
