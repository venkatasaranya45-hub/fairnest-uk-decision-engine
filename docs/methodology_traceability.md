# Methodology and traceability

## Research design
The study uses a pragmatic, secondary-data feasibility design combining official statistics, regulation, peer-reviewed scholarship, first-party provider data and competitor interfaces.

No participants or personal datasets are used in the completed study.

## Evidence hierarchy
1. Official, regulatory and first-party institutional sources.
2. Peer-reviewed scholarship.
3. Specialist market intelligence.
4. Live commercial platforms for competitor analysis.

## Evidence-to-design chain
- Affordability -> weekly rent, contract total and known booking cash are separated.
- Information asymmetry -> source URLs, check dates and visible missing fields are retained.
- Contract context -> halls, private PBSA and assured private-tenancy contexts are not treated as identical.
- Preference heterogeneity -> ranking uses user-adjustable weights.
- Commercial governance -> paid status is excluded from the organic Fit Score.
- Data minimisation -> protected characteristics are not required for ranking.

## Reliability controls
The evidence model records source URLs and check dates, separates sourced values from managerial assumptions, retains incomplete records rather than silently imputing favourable values, and applies a structured competitor audit.

## Limitations
The Coventry listing sample is a prototype sample rather than a census. Accommodation prices and availability are dynamic. Financial assumptions require live validation before commercial launch.
