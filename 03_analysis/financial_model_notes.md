# Financial model notes

## Purpose
The model tests whether a lean Coventry pilot can approach operating sustainability. It is a pre-launch feasibility model, not an investment forecast.

## Managerial assumptions
- single verified provider profile: £29/month;
- Provider Pro account: £149/month;
- optional student planning/export product: £7.99 one-off;
- fixed launch cost: £5,900;
- monthly operating cost: £1,200 in Month 1 rising to £1,450 in Month 12.

These prices are **not** presented as observed willingness to pay.

## Exclusions
Founder salary, corporation tax, VAT treatment and financing cost are excluded from the academic pilot model.

## Scenario conclusion
All three Year-1 scenarios remain cash-negative. The base case approaches monthly operating break-even by Month 12 but does not recover launch cost in Year 1.

This is treated as a feasibility finding rather than hidden through optimistic assumptions.

## Decision implication
A live pilot would require:
- a capped funding buffer;
- provider-acquisition gates;
- measured conversion and retention;
- evidence-refresh cost measurement;
- go/no-go decisions before geographic expansion.
