# Artefact validation summary

The artefact was evaluated through scenario-based testing rather than participant testing.

## Test categories
- budget sensitivity;
- booking-cash sensitivity;
- missing-data handling;
- private-tenancy context;
- university-hall context;
- user-weight changes;
- evidence provenance;
- paid-status independence;
- sponsor summary;
- legal-advice boundary.

The detailed test cases and outcomes are in `test_record.csv`.

## What the tests establish
They show that the prototype implements the stated decision rules consistently.

## What the tests do not establish
They do not demonstrate:
- user satisfaction;
- improved real-world accommodation outcomes;
- provider willingness to pay;
- conversion;
- retention;
- national usability.

Those questions require future live validation.
