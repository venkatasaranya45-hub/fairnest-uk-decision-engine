# Limitations and future validation

## Current limitations
1. Coventry evidence is a proof-of-concept sample, not a census of every available room.
2. Accommodation prices and availability are dynamic.
3. The project does not contain primary student usability evidence.
4. Provider willingness to pay is not observed.
5. Customer-acquisition cost and retention are not measured.
6. Legal prompts are informational and cannot replace individual advice.
7. The Fit Score reflects stated preferences and available evidence, not objective property quality.

## Required validation before scale
- structured usability sessions under approved ethics;
- provider interviews or commercial pilot data;
- measured data-refresh effort per listing;
- conversion and retention evidence;
- customer-acquisition cost;
- complaint/correction workflow testing;
- independent review of legal-boundary wording;
- repeat the evidence process in a second city before national expansion.

## Expansion gate
FairNest should expand only when evidence shows that the additional city can be sourced and maintained without weakening provenance, user trust or unit economics.
