# Ethics scope and approval

## Approval context
The completed FairNest UK study sits under Coventry University's **7001SOM low-risk block approval P192092**.

## Completed-study scope
- Secondary research only.
- No participant recruitment.
- No interviews, surveys or focus groups are reported.
- No personal dataset is analysed.
- The artefact is a non-transactional prototype rather than a live accommodation-booking service.

## Ethical controls applied
The project therefore focuses its ethical controls on:
- accurate representation of public evidence;
- explicit treatment of uncertainty and missing data;
- non-deceptive interface design;
- separation of paid status from organic ranking;
- data minimisation;
- exclusion of protected characteristics from ranking;
- clear legal-advice boundaries;
- source provenance and evidence-check dates.

## Future live-pilot requirement
A future implementation involving users, enquiries or provider data would require the relevant ethics/data-protection review, documented lawful basis, privacy information and retention controls before collection begins.

This file records the approval context and scope used in the final submission; it does not extend the approved study beyond that scope.
