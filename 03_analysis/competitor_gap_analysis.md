# Competitor gap analysis

The competitor review intentionally avoids a straw-man claim that existing services do not compare accommodation.

## What established services already do well
- Student.com: detailed cross-property comparison.
- University Living: large student inventory and booking-policy signals.
- Rightmove: broad property search plus international-student guidance.
- Student SpareRoom: large classified marketplace with safety guidance.
- Coventry University: strong first-party fees, location and payment information for its own halls.
- UKCISA / UCAS: specialist guidance and budgeting support.

## Residual integration gap
Across the reviewed services, the strongest defensible opportunity is the integration of:
- weekly rent;
- total contract cost;
- known booking cash;
- agreement context;
- source provenance;
- evidence-check dates;
- explicit missing-data treatment;
- user-controlled weights;
- parent/sponsor decision support.

## Competitive discipline
FairNest should not claim exclusive access to listings or unique possession of general safety advice. Its differentiation is the **governance of the comparison process**.
