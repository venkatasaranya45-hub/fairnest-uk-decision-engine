# Project overview

## Business issue
The project addresses the difficulty of comparing student accommodation when providers present different pricing units, payment schedules, contract forms, evidence signals and location information.

The central business problem is **decision friction**, not lack of inventory.

## Proposed solution
FairNest UK is an independent decision-support layer that standardises:
- weekly rent;
- total contract cost;
- known booking or move-in cash;
- bills;
- commute;
- agreement context;
- source provenance;
- evidence completeness.

## Initial market
Coventry was selected as a deliberately demanding pilot because it has a large student market and substantial PBSA supply. A relatively well-supplied market is useful for testing whether decision quality creates value even when scarcity is not the main problem.

## Intended users
Primary:
- international students making pre-arrival accommodation decisions.

Secondary:
- parents/sponsors involved in payment decisions;
- accommodation providers seeking structured visibility;
- later-stage university/student-union partners.

## Commercial boundary
The pilot keeps core comparison and safety/legal signposting free. Revenue assumptions test provider subscriptions and optional planning/export tools. No willingness-to-pay evidence is claimed.
