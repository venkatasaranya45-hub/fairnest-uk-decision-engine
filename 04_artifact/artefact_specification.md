# Artefact specification

## Product name
FairNest UK Decision Engine

## Purpose
To demonstrate how the research findings can be translated into a decision-support interface for international students comparing Coventry accommodation.

## Main screens/functions
1. Discover & Compare
2. Total Cost / booking-cash interpretation
3. Contract & Legal Check
4. Evidence Provenance
5. Parent/Sponsor Summary
6. Fit Score / preference controls

## Functional principles
- evidence remains visible beside the recommendation;
- source links and check dates are exposed;
- missing data are visible;
- user weights can be changed;
- accommodation context is not collapsed into one legal category;
- no paid-status ranking;
- no legal or safety guarantee.

## Acceptance
The evidence-to-feature mapping and scenario test record are maintained in the companion CSV files.
