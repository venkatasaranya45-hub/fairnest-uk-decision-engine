# Research questions and objectives

## Aim
To evaluate the commercial, operational and responsible-business feasibility of FairNest UK as a platform that standardises total contract cost, known booking cash, contractual context, commute and source provenance for international students choosing Coventry accommodation.

## Research questions
**RQ1.** What decision challenges and information gaps do international students face when securing UK accommodation?

**RQ2.** Which standardised cost and verification signals make comparison across housing types meaningful?

**RQ3.** Where do existing platforms and university guidance leave residual gaps in surfacing those signals consistently?

**RQ4.** What legal, ethical and data-sourcing safeguards should govern a pilot?

## Objectives
1. Profile the accommodation decision context using official and scholarly evidence.
2. Audit current accommodation channels and competitor capabilities.
3. Specify a transparent comparison framework.
4. Build and scenario-test a functional secondary-data artefact.
5. Assess commercial, operational, ethical and regulatory feasibility.

## Scope
The completed study is a Coventry proof of concept. It does not claim national validation or observed willingness to pay.
