# Data dictionary

## Coventry listing dataset
- `ID`: stable prototype record identifier.
- `Accommodation`: room/property label.
- `Provider`: accommodation provider or discovery platform.
- `Provider type`: university hall, PBSA or aggregator.
- `Room type`: en-suite, studio, shared or unspecified.
- `Weekly rent (£)`: published weekly headline rent.
- `Contract weeks`: published contract length.
- `Contract total (£)`: published or derived contract total.
- `Move-in cash captured (£)`: known amount captured from the source; not necessarily the complete eventual pre-arrival liability.
- `Walk to Coventry Uni (min)`: published/captured walking-time field.
- `Bills`: whether bills are included or require checking.
- `Evidence completeness`: prototype traceability indicator, not a safety probability.
- `Contract context`: agreement category or classification warning.
- `Evidence checked`: date on which evidence was checked.
- `Primary source` / `Supporting source`: source URLs.
- `Notes`: important caveat or interpretation.

## Competitor audit
Compares channels on comparison functionality, cost detail, location, contractual guidance, trust signals, source provenance, tenancy-context logic and booking-cash normalisation.

## Legal and governance rules
Maps regulatory or ethical constraints to prototype controls. It is a design aid and does not provide individual legal advice.

## Financial model
Separates sourced market facts from managerial assumptions. Scenario outputs are feasibility tests rather than investment forecasts.
